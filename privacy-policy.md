## English

CYCURID TECNOLOGIES LTD. ("we", "us", "our") provides an application that connects to Atlassian Jira. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when users install, authorize, and use our app.

### 1. Scope
This policy applies to information processed by our Jira-connected app and related support operations.

### 2. Information We Process
We may process:
- Account and workspace metadata (for example, Atlassian account ID, site URL, project metadata)

### 3. Purposes of Processing
We process information to:
- Provide and operate app features requested by users
- Sync, display, transform, or analyze Jira data based on user actions
- Maintain service integrity, security, and reliability
- Provide support and incident response

### 4. Legal Bases (where applicable)
Depending on applicable law, legal bases may include contract performance, legitimate interests (such as security and service improvement), legal obligations, and consent where required.

### 5. Data Sharing and Subprocessors
We do not sell personal data. We may share data with trusted service providers supporting hosting, monitoring, security, analytics, or support operations under contractual data protection obligations, or when required by law.

### 6. International Data Transfers
If data is transferred across borders, we implement reasonable safeguards required by applicable law.

### 7. Data Retention
We retain data only as long as necessary to provide services, maintain security records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. Data is deleted or anonymized when no longer required.

### 8. Security
We maintain administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

### 9. Your Rights
Subject to local law, users may request access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, or restriction of processing. Where consent is the basis, users may withdraw consent.
